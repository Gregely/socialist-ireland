# What had to go right

Every good thing in this section depends on some of the conditions below. Each has an identifier, which the timeline uses to tag what each milestone needed. **There are 37 of them, and the best case needs every one.** They are not independent: a mild reaction abroad makes it easier for multinationals to stay, and high participation makes farmers likelier to negotiate. But nor are they likely together. No probabilities are given, because the research cannot support them. The honest summary is that this is the republic if almost everything breaks its way.

## The economy model's settings

The best case moves every uncertainty in the [pressure explorer](#/economy/pressure) to the favourable end of its researched range, and no further. The values are in `content/aisling/settings.json`; a test fails if any of them leaves its range.

### S-mne_exit — A quarter of the multinationals leave, not half
Three-quarters of the multinationals' local spending stays, under published charters (Art. 52). Plausible at the favourable end because 42.9% of Irish goods exports go to the United States, so for many of these firms an Irish base outside the EU still serves their main market ([13 §4.1](research/13-leaving-the-eu.md)). The model's tipping point at the other best-case settings is about 44%.

### S-capital_flight — Only 5% of deposits leave during the notice period
Capital controls are imposed in the first week, as Cyprus and Greece did inside the euro, and are believed. Greece lost about 23% in months without them ([12 §7.4](research/12-economy.md)).

### S-devaluation — The punt falls 15%, not 30%
The fall is the size of sterling's in 1992, the mildest case in the research, rather than Nomura's 30% estimate for an Irish break-up ([13 §2.3](research/13-leaving-the-eu.md)).

### S-trade_terms — Near-free trade with the EU and the UK
Agreements close to tariff-free, of the UK–EU kind, with subsidy-control terms the co-operative sector can live with ([13 §4.3](research/13-leaving-the-eu.md)).

### S-herd_reduction — The herd falls by 35% over ten years, by agreement
A design target rather than a favourable extreme: the reduction the ecological plan needs, negotiated with farmers through the Land and Farm Transition Board, with every farm household's income guaranteed (Art. 67).

### S-hours_reduction — A four-day week by year 10
Working hours fall by a fifth, the top of the explorer's range, as Article 72 requires when productivity rises.

### S-energy_price — World energy prices 15% below 2025 levels
Inside the range set by the 2021–22 swing in Ireland's energy import bill ([12 §7.2](research/12-economy.md); CSO TSA06).

### S-participation — Nearly half of residents take part in local assemblies and planning
At the favourable end of the explorer's range. In the model this is what keeps public investment from being wasted (FM-06, FM-08). It is also the weakest-evidenced link in the model.

## Safeguards that held

### G-FM-11 — The windfall was ring-fenced in year one
Corporation tax above trend went into the Social Wealth Fund before any change of system (Arts. 60, 114), so the State's budget never depended on the firms that might leave.

### G-FM-15 — Every emergency ended on time, and the capital controls lapsed
The 30-day limit and the Citizens' Chamber's control of extensions held (Art. 90), and the Assembly let the controls on corporate transfers lapse at a six-monthly review (Art. 61.5) rather than keeping them for a decade, as Iceland did.

### G-FM-06 — Hard budgets held
No enterprise or council was rescued without a published vote and its cost (Art. 54).

### G-FM-01 — Office stayed ordinary
The Pay and Privileges Panel, chosen by lot, kept officials on a skilled worker's pay (Arts. 28, 100), and the accounts stayed open (Art. 59).

### G-FM-02 — The party never took command
Under the default settlement, the party held no seat or veto on any planning body, board, bank or security service (Arts. 30.2, 86), and its internal platforms stayed protected (Art. 31).

### G-FM-08 — Local money stayed local
At least a third of every county's capital budget was spent by its local assemblies, year after year (Art. 57).

### G-FM-09 — Co-operatives kept their doors open
New workers became members after a year's probation (Art. 50), so the co-operatives did not harden into closed shops of insiders.

### G-FM-16 — The Ecological Authority's budgets bound the plan
The physical budgets were set independently and enforced (Arts. 63–64). The plan was amended twice to fit them.

### G-FM-18 — Care was counted and paid
The National Care Service was built on public-sector terms, and the time-use survey put unpaid care into every plan (Art. 71).

### G-FM-17 — Prices moved by published rule, and no one queued past anyone else
When the punt fell, price formulas passed rises on by rule with compensation built in, and nothing was rationed by connection (Art. 58).

### G-FM-03 — The term limits held
The most popular minister of the decade left office on time (Art. 28; the [term-limits stress test](#/stress/term-limits)).

### G-FM-04 — The security services stayed under oversight
The Security Oversight Committee, partly chosen by lot, had full access and used it (Art. 85).

## Failure modes that did not happen

### A-FM-10 — No foreign-backed campaign of sabotage or subversion
The republic had enemies, but none of them financed violence on a scale ordinary policing could not handle.

### A-FM-12 — No restoration from inside
No faction of officials or managers quietly rebuilt private ownership for itself, as happened in the Soviet Union's last years.

### A-FM-13 — No ruinous split on the left
The parties, platforms and unions argued constantly, and none of them walked out of the democratic order.

### A-FM-14 — The national question did not swallow everything
The border checks, the farmers and the North were argued over hard, without becoming a nationalist crusade on either side.

### A-FM-05 — Planning did not seize up
The Investment Council's negotiations produced a plan on time in each five-year cycle, without the shortages planned economies became known for.

### A-FM-07 — Work did not go dead
Shorter hours and a share in decisions kept people working well. There was no "they pretend to pay us" malaise.

## Lucky breaks

### L1 — A mild reaction abroad
The European Central Bank kept lending to solvent Irish banks during the notice period. No government imposed sanctions. The EU and the UK negotiated in good faith.

### L2 — The multinationals' main market is America
Most of the large firms stayed because their exports go to the United States, and the charter terms were acceptable to them.

### L3 — Cheap energy
World energy prices stayed low through the first decade.

### L4 — No world recession in the first five years
No global downturn, pandemic or war hit during the notice period and the changeover.

### L5 — The losers accepted the result
The 44% who voted against the Constitution organised as a loyal opposition (associations, independent candidates, newspapers), not as a boycott or a resistance.

### L6 — The farmers chose to negotiate
The farmers' organisations went to the Land and Farm Transition Board rather than to the barricades.

### L7 — No one was killed over the border
There were protests at the crossings, and some damage, but no deaths.

### L8 — Participation did not fade
The local assemblies stayed full after the novelty wore off, unlike Porto Alegre's budget after 2004 ([06 §9](research/06-positive-models.md)).

### L9 — The builders and nurses stayed
Skilled workers did not leave for Britain in large numbers, though the Common Travel Area would have let them.

### L10 — Good weather in the transition years
No ruined harvest or windless winter came while the herd was being cut and the grid rebuilt.

### L11 — Britain stayed a good neighbour
London kept the Common Travel Area and agreed to keep checks on goods away from the border, though nothing obliged it to.
