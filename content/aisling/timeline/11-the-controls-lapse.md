At the twelfth six-monthly review (Art. 61.5), the Assembly declines to renew the remaining controls on corporate transfers. The Central Bank reports that reserves cover more than three years of essential imports. The Bank's minutes, published four weeks later, show the vote on its advice was five to two.

## Where it could have gone wrong

Iceland's "temporary" controls lasted from 2008 to 2017 ([13 §3](research/13-leaving-the-eu.md)). Controls that are ordinary law can simply be renewed for ever, as they are in the [capital flight](#/stress/capital-flight) test's tighter-controls path.
