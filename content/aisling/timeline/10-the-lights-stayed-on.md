A week of still, freezing weather in January coincides with a fault on a gas interconnector. The Executive declares an emergency limited to the electricity system. The free household energy allowance is protected (Art. 24); data centres and the largest industrial users are curtailed first by published rule. The community wind and battery co-operatives that county councils have backed since year 2 (Art. 49) carry the peak in four counties. The emergency ends on day 19 (Art. 90).

By year 10, wind generation is roughly two and a half times what it was in 2024. Gas has become a backup. Coal and peat are no longer burned (the illustrative energy picture, [Flows](#/economy/flows)).

## Where it could have gone wrong

The same week, in the [harvest and energy shock](#/stress/harvest-energy-shock) test, forces a choice about who gets the power. An emergency that did not end on time would have been the first step towards entrenched emergency powers (FM-15).
