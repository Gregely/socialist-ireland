The Constitution is adopted by 56% on a high turnout, after the second Dáil passes it unchanged (Art. 111). The ballot paper said in plain words that it meant leaving the European Union and the euro (Art. 111.2). Nobody can say afterwards that they did not know.

The losing side does not boycott the new order. Within a month there are three new associations campaigning for a return to the EU, two newspapers attacking the transition every morning, and a slate of independent candidates for the first council elections. They are free to do all of it (Arts. 10–12), and that is what makes the result stick.

## Where it could have gone wrong

A narrower vote, and a minority that treats the result as illegitimate. Support for EU membership was 82% in 2025 and 2026 ([08 §10](research/08-ireland-today.md)). See the stress test's opening step.
