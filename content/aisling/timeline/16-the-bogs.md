The drained bogs of the midlands are wet again. Curlew are breeding on the Bog of Allen for the first time in a generation; there are boardwalks at Lough Boora and in the old cutaways of Offaly and Westmeath. By year 10, greenhouse gas emissions were about a third below 2025, at roughly 33 Mt a year. That is within the yearly average of the 2026–30 carbon budget, but still above the 2031–35 average of 30 Mt ([Flows](#/economy/flows)). Rewetting and the second decade of the energy plan close that gap.

## Where it could have gone wrong

The Ecological Authority's veto and the farmers' Board could have deadlocked, with no rule for who wins ([open questions](#/open)). The physical budget held because both sides kept negotiating.
