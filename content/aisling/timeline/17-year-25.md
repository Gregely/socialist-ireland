The children born in the year of the vote are voting now. They have never paid rent to a landlord, seen a hospital trolley or worked a five-day week. They complain about the Assembly, the buses and the price of coffee. A quarter of them think the old Ireland must have been more exciting, and they say so in newspapers the State does not own.

The economy model at its best-case settings: debt about 36% of GNI*, inflation 2%, unemployment 3% with the Job Guarantee, and pay per hour worked about 40% above today's.

## Where it could have gone wrong

Everywhere, and the rest of this site says how. The best case is what the safeguards are for.
