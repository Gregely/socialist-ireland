Public and co-operative completions pass 20,000 a year, double the 10,571 social homes of 2025, inside a building industry that already completed 36,284 homes that year ([12 §5.3](research/12-economy.md)). The Housing Authority builds to its own pattern book: three and four storeys, deep windows, shared gardens, heat pumps, a creche and a shop unit on every block (Art. 69). One per cent of every scheme's budget goes on art and amenity (Art. 68.2), so the first estate in Clondalkin opens with a mosaic by a local school and a covered bandstand.

Rents are set at cost. Tenants elect each estate's council, which shares in management (Art. 69.2). The waiting list starts, at last, to fall.

## Where it could have gone wrong

A budget squeeze could have frozen building and wages, as in the [general strike](#/stress/general-strike) test. A construction workforce leaving for Britain could have stalled it, or investment captured by contractors with no hard budget behind them (FM-06).
