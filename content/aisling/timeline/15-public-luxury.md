Every county town has a library open until ten at night, with a workshop and a recording studio; most have a public bath and sauna, many in restored Victorian buildings. The local assemblies, spending their third of the capital budget (Art. 57), have chosen parks, playgrounds, rebuilt market halls and the restoration of shopfronts more often than anything else. The Arts and Design Council funds a commission for every new public building (Arts. 25, 68.2).

The Public Media Trust's drama department makes more Irish-language television than at any time in the State's history, and some of it is very good.

## Where it could have gone wrong

Public luxury is the first thing cut in a budget squeeze, and the first thing to look shabby if it is not maintained: the grey socialism of FM-17. The [general strike](#/stress/general-strike) test begins with exactly such a squeeze.
