At midnight on the first of January the punt replaces the euro, one for one, for every wage, rent, mortgage and deposit under Irish law (Art. 104.2). Card payments switch overnight. Euro cash is exchanged over four weeks at post offices. The notes carry Rosc-style designs chosen by a citizens' assembly.

The punt falls 15% in the first month and then steadies. The essential-imports schedule (Art. 106.2), foreign-currency reserves covering about two years of energy, food and medicines, and cheap world energy carry the first winter. **Inflation peaks at 5%** and is back near 2% within three years (the economy model, best-case settings). Fuel and imported food rise by published formula, with compensation built in for low incomes (Art. 58.2).

## Where it could have gone wrong

A fall of 30% or more, dearer energy, or reserves run down by capital flight. On the model's central settings inflation reaches 8.5% on exit day, and the external accounts break within three years ([pressure explorer](#/economy/pressure)).
