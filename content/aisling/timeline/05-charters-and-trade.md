Three-quarters of the multinationals' local spending stays. The pharmaceutical and medical-device firms that sell mainly to America sign charters fixing their tax, their workers' rights and their share of the water and energy budgets (Art. 52). The charters are published in full, and two of them are amended after hearings at which the workplace councils speak first.

Near-tariff-free agreements with the EU and with Britain follow. The EU's subsidy-control chapter allows support with *"an equity rationale"*, and the co-operative development fund is rebuilt to fit it. Because the chapter touches Part V, it goes through two Assemblies and a referendum (Arts. 104.4, 109), and passes.

## Where it could have gone wrong

If half the multinationals had left, on the model's central settings the external balance breaks in year 5 ([capital flight](#/stress/capital-flight)). Without a trade deal, beef and dairy face tariffs such as 12.8% plus €303.4 per 100 kg ([13 §4.2](research/13-leaving-the-eu.md)).
