Notice under Article 50 is given the morning after the vote. In the first week the Assembly passes published, temporary capital controls: €100 a day in cash, approval for transfers abroad above €2,000 a month, and wages, card payments and imports untouched. Five per cent of household deposits leave before they bite. The windfall corporation tax is already ring-fenced in the Social Wealth Fund (Arts. 60, 114).

The European Central Bank goes on lending to solvent Irish banks; the Commission reserves its position. The first Citizens' Chamber, 120 residents chosen by lot, sits in the old Seanad chamber, and the first local assemblies meet in parish halls and GAA clubhouses (Arts. 35, 39).

## Where it could have gone wrong

A two-year public notice is an invitation to a run on the banks. Without early controls, or if the ECB had capped emergency lending as it did to Greece in 2015, the State would have been forced into a bank holiday on worse terms ([13 §3](research/13-leaving-the-eu.md)).
