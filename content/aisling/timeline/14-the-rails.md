The Western Rail Corridor reopens between Athenry, Tuam and Claremorris, one of the reinstatements the All-Island Strategic Rail Review recommended in 2024 ([14](research/14-geography-sources.md)). Rail to Shannon airport follows. Public transport is free at the point of use (Art. 68); in every town of more than a thousand people a bus runs at least hourly, the standard Article 24 sets for making a car unnecessary.

## Where it could have gone wrong

Big projects are where planning fails most expensively: overruns, investment hunger and contractors without hard budgets (FM-05, FM-06; [12 §8.4](research/12-economy.md)).
