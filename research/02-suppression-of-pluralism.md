# 02 — The suppression of pluralism: Luxemburg, the soviets, the factory committees, Kronstadt

## 1. Why this matters most

Most later failure modes (the new class, the cult, the security state) became *possible* only once rival socialist parties, independent soviets and workplace committees were gone. The years 1918–21 are where the exits closed. The Bolsheviks' defenders point to civil war, foreign intervention, famine and sabotage. Their critics point to choices made before those pressures peaked, or kept after they had passed. Both sides have evidence. The design question is how to survive real emergencies without making emergency measures permanent.

**The pattern.** Each step was justified as temporary or as a response to emergency. Each removed a channel through which the governing party could be corrected from below: first the national parliament, then rival parties in the soviets, then workplace self-management, then independent soviet elections, then debate *inside* the ruling party. By 1921 the only remaining check on the leadership was the leadership.

## 3. Rosa Luxemburg — *The Russian Revolution* (written 1918, published 1922)

Written in prison in Breslau. Published posthumously by Paul Levi in 1922. Full text on [marxists.org](https://www.marxists.org/archive/luxemburg/1918/russian-revolution/index.htm), Bertram Wolfe translation.

Luxemburg **supported** the October seizure of power and praised the Bolsheviks for daring. Her critique comes from a revolutionary, not from a liberal. Key passages, checked against [chapter 6, "The Problem of Dictatorship"](https://www.marxists.org/archive/luxemburg/1918/russian-revolution/ch06.htm):

- *"Freedom only for the supporters of the government, only for the members of one party – however numerous they may be – is no freedom at all. Freedom is always and exclusively freedom for the one who thinks differently."*
- Her prediction of what follows from suppressing elections and the press: *"Without general elections, without unrestricted freedom of press and assembly,"* … *"public life gradually falls asleep"*, ending in *"not the dictatorship of the proletariat but only the dictatorship of a handful of politicians."*
- She treats broad democratic public life as the "life element" of proletarian rule, not as a bourgeois luxury to be postponed.

She also criticised the Bolshevik land policy (land distributed to peasants as private plots) and their policy on national self-determination. Both are relevant to Ireland (agriculture; the national question). See the [chapter list](https://www.marxists.org/archive/luxemburg/1918/russian-revolution/index.htm).

**The strongest reply to Luxemburg.** Lenin and Trotsky argued, and their defenders still argue, that in a civil war with foreign intervention, open elections and a free press would have handed power to the Whites. On this view, democracy was a casualty of the war and not of Bolshevik doctrine. The counter-evidence is that several restrictions came *before* the civil war peaked (the January 1918 dissolution, the March 1918 one-man management decree), and others were *tightened* after the Whites were defeated (Kronstadt and the ban on factions, both March 1921). The site should present both readings.

## 4. Kronstadt in particular

- The sailors had been among the Bolsheviks' strongest supporters in 1917. In 1921 they demanded "soviets without Bolsheviks" (Britannica's summary of their slogans), free soviet elections, and freedom of speech for left parties and anarchists ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/print/article/323794)).
- Bolshevik defenders said that the 1921 garrison was different from the 1917 one, and that émigré Whites hoped to use the revolt. Anarchist and libertarian-socialist accounts reject this ([libcom](https://libcom.org/history/1921-the-kronstadt-rebellion); [Anarchist Library](https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/shawn-hattingh-the-kronstadt-rebellion-still-significant-90-years-on)). The site should present the dispute and not settle it.
- **The lesson that is not disputed.** A government that claimed authority *from* the soviets used force against people demanding free soviet elections. Once that happens, the soviet form is no longer a source of legitimacy; the army is.

## 5. The factory committees

- In 1917, factory committees went beyond wage demands to challenge managers' authority over hiring, production and accounts ([Brinton](https://www.marxists.org/archive/brinton/1970/workers-control/02.htm)).
- Carl Beck Papers scholarship finds that the Bolsheviks held at least three different conceptions of "workers' control" at once. Lenin's emphasised *accounting and supervision*, not workers running enterprises ([Beck](https://carlbeckpapers.pitt.edu/ojs/cbp/article/view/21/20)).
- Historians disagree about why the turn to one-man management happened. Some point to ideology and centralism; others to the demands of civil war ([Avrich](https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/paul-avrich-bolshevik-opposition-to-lenin.muse); [ATC, "Of Lenin and Leninism"](https://www.marxists.org/history/etol/newspape/atc/5218.html)).

**Design lesson.** Workplace self-management needs a constitutional floor that no government can take away in the name of efficiency or emergency. Enterprise-level democracy should also be federated through its *own* structures and not only through the state or a party. And there is a real coordination problem (one plant cannot decide alone what the whole economy needs), so that tension has to be *designed for* and not wished away.

## 6. What this suggests

1. **Entrenched political pluralism among socialists at minimum.** Ideally it should be broader (see the party question in Phase 2). It should be entrenched so that a simple majority cannot remove it.
2. **No "temporary" suspension of elections.** Emergency powers should be time-limited, need supermajority renewal, be reviewable by courts and be subject to a recall referendum.
3. **No military or police role in internal political disputes** unless the courts authorise it.
4. **Party-internal rights.** If a leading party exists, the constitution should guarantee the right to form platforms or factions inside it, since the 1921 ban was the hinge for everything after.
5. **Workplace self-management and a federation of workers' councils protected by the constitution**, with its own channel to national decision-making.
