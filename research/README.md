## Method and honesty notes

- **Sources.** These come from web searches run in October 2026. Primary texts (marxists.org, Wikisource, court judgments, CSO, EPA, Revenue) are preferred where they could be reached. Some claims rest on secondary or partisan sources (libcom, the Anarchist Library, Trotskyist and Austrian-school sites). Where that is the case the text says so, because these sources have a stake in the story they tell.
- **Quotations.** Every direct quotation was checked against the linked text during this research pass. Nothing was quoted from memory. Where I could not check a famous line against its source, it is paraphrased or marked as unverified.
- **Figures.** Contested numbers (for example Stasi informants, Great Terror executions, Fagor job losses, Cuban consultation meetings) are given as ranges, with the disagreement stated.
- **Currency.** Contemporary Irish data is the latest I could find as of October 2026. Fast-moving items (homelessness, the triple lock bill, unity polling) will date quickly, and the site should show "as of" dates.
- **Gaps that remain.** These are listed at the end of `SYNTHESIS.md`.
